# 🌌 Project: Voidstrike - The Crown Gate Outbreak
### A D&D 5e / Solo Leveling Campaign Setting for Spiritual Education (Ubuntu)

This repository contains the core mechanics, system parameters, and narrative lore for a custom tabletop campaign. It merges high-octane modern hunter progression with the philosophical architecture of **Ubuntu** (*"I am because we are"*).

## 🛠️ System Overview: The Ubuntu Engine

In standard settings, players level up by gathering personal experience points (XP) or tracking milestones. In this campaign, individual accumulation is a strategic vulnerability. Progression is tied to a shared ecosystem.

### The Ubuntu Tracker
* **The Party Pool:** The party shares a single **Ubuntu Score (US)** starting at 0. 
* **Mechanic:** The party wins or loses as a single unit. Individual levels are discarded; the group ascends through global **Threat Tiers** together.

| Tier | Name | Rank Match | Group Bonuses Unlocked |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | Broken Shards | E-Rank | Base stats only. Disadvantage on isolation saves. |
| **1** | Interconnected | D/C-Rank | +1 to all Saving Throws per adjacent ally. |
| **2** | Shared Burden | B-Rank | *Shield Transfer* unlocked (absorb ally damage as a reaction). |
| **3** | The Vanguard | A-Rank | *Resonance Strike* (allies can chain combo actions). |
| **4** | Cosmic Crown | S-Rank | *Heavens Opened* mythic field effect available. |

## 👤 Core Character Blueprint: Saint Stephen
*Hellenistic Protomartyr (Special Mythic Companion/NPC Block)*

Stephen does not use a traditional character sheet. His statistics are architectural, shifting according to the layout and moral alignment of the party.

* **Armor Class:** 10 + Current Ubuntu Score (Max 22)
* **Hit Points:** Distributed among the party via the *Ubuntu Matrix*.
* **Speed:** 30 ft.

### ⚔️ Special Combat Mechanic: Blind Martial Gnosis
Stephen can deliberately sacrifice his physical sight to enter an unshakeable state of defensive flow.

* **Activation:** Free action. Stephen ties a coarse linen cloth over his eyes, inflicting the **Blinded** condition on himself.
* **Spatial Gnosis:** He loses all traditional vision but instantly gains **60 feet of Blindsight**. He cannot be surprised, and unseen attackers do not gain advantage on him.
* **Flawless Proficiency:** While blinded, Stephen treats any object, improvised weapon, or unarmed strike as a weapon with which he has absolute mastery. 
* **The Logic of the Matrix:** His attacks use his **Wisdom modifier** instead of Strength or Dexterity. When attacking an enemy, he does not strike to destroy; he senses the spatial flaws in their posture and allows the weight of their own momentum to disable them.

## 🎭 Act 1 Catalyst: The Neon Crossroads

### Scene Setup: The Safe Zone Alleyway
*The DM reads or copies the following prompt during the transition between the initial Rank-D Gate dungeon and the urban hub:*

> The neon signs of the metro district hum with a persistent, low-frequency buzz, casting sharp magenta and cyan shadows against the wet brick walls of the alley. Your party ducks past the automated vending machines to avoid the corporate hunter scouts. 
> 
> Sitting on an overturned plastic crate is a young Black child wearing a faded hoodie. In his lap sits a heavily dog-eared, stylized graphic novel titled *Legends of the First Witnesses*. He looks up, his eyes darting from his pages to the ancient, dust-covered linen tunic and the faint, geometric Greek lettering floating around the shoulders of your companion.

### Core Dialogue Interaction
**Little Steph:** "Yo... no way. You're the first one, right? The guy who stood up to the whole council? My grandma named me after you. She said your name means the Crown, and that you didn't drop it even when the stones started flying."

**Saint Stephen:** *(His posture remains rigid, a cold, Stoic anchor amidst the flickering city lights. He analyzes the ink drawings of his own execution in Jerusalem.)* "They turned my final breath into an ink-bound chronicle? They sell my execution on your street corners?"

**Little Steph:** "It’s not just a comic, man. It’s how we know we can survive when the system traps us. You didn't hate the people throwing the rocks. You showed them that they couldn't break your soul because you were holding onto something bigger than their walls. You stood up so the rest of the community could run."
