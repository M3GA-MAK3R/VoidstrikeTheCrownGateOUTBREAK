When the party travels through the modern metropolis outside the gate, roll a **d6** to trigger localized spiritual or kinetic friction. These encounters explicitly test your physical Ubuntu Tracker and Stephen's Blind Martial Gnosis.

| d6 | Encounter Name | Threat Type | Operational Mechanics (Physical Tabletop) |

# 👔 Boss Encounter Block: Saul, Corporate Vanguard

Saul represents rigid institutional power, hyper-individualistic efficiency, and legalistic tracking. He views Stephen as an "unregistered anomaly" threatening market stability.

* **Medium Humanoid (S-Rank Tracker)**
* **Armor Class:** 18 (Sleek Weave Armor)
* **Hit Points:** 145 
* **Speed:** 45 ft.

### Traits
* **Market Dominance:** Saul deals an extra **2d6 Piercing damage** to any character who has not received aid or healing from an ally in the last round. He preys entirely on isolation.
* **Unshakeable Contract:** Saul is immune to being charmed, frightened, or moved against his will by magical effects.

### Actions
* **Multiattack:** Saul makes two swift strikes with his *Monofilament Energy Blade* (+8 to hit, Reach 5 ft). *Damage:* 2d8 + 4 Slashing damage per strike.
* **Systemic Extraction:** Saul targets one PC within 30 feet and forces a DC 15 Strength or Charisma saving throw. On a failure, a kinetic force barrier slams down, dragging that PC 20 feet away from the party into a solo duel containment ring.
* **The Stone Gavel (Recharge 5–6):** Saul calls down an orbital kinetic strike on a 10-ft radius spot. Any character caught inside takes 4d10 Bludgeoning damage, halved on a DC 16 Dexterity save. *Ubuntu Integration:* If Stephen is inside the radius, allies can use their reaction to leap into the zone, taking the damage for him to supercharge the party's *Ubuntu Matrix*.

### Reactions
* **Red Tape:** When an ally attempts to use a help action, throw a shield, or intercept damage for another player, Saul can use his reaction to force a DC 14 Intelligence save. On a failure, the collaborative action is canceled, forcing the players to rethink their physical grid spacing.
